# Retail Sales, Customers & Inventory Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This is a internship based on project **Retail Analytics project** developed using Microsoft Excel to analyze retail sales, customer information, and inventory performance.

The project converts raw retail data into meaningful business information using Excel formulas, PivotTables, PivotCharts, and an interactive dashboard.

The analysis focuses on three major areas:

- Sales Performance
- Customer Analysis
- Inventory Management

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall retail sales performance.
- Identify high-performing products and categories.
- Analyze monthly sales trends.
- Understand customer characteristics and visit patterns.
- Analyze payment preferences.
- Monitor inventory stock availability.
- Identify products requiring reorder.
- Analyze inventory value by category.
- Create a management-oriented Excel dashboard.

## 🔍 Key Insights

### Sales Performance

- Total sales amounted to **₹1,42,946** from 1,000 transactions.
- **Cleaning** generated the highest category sales at **₹36,120**.
- **August** recorded the highest monthly sales at **₹39,953**.
- **Surf Excel** was the top-selling product at **₹18,315**.
- UPI generated approximately **₹73,334**, representing about **51% of sales value**.
- Cash generated approximately **₹69,612**, representing about **49% of sales value**.
- Beverage recorded the highest quantity sold by category at **823 units**.

### Customer Analysis

- The dataset contains **300 customers**.
- Female customers numbered **154**, compared with **146 male customers**.
- Customers **above 30 years** formed the largest age group with **228 customers**.
- **Regular customers** were the largest visit-type group with **104 customers**.
- Cash was the preferred payment method among **175 customers**, compared with **125 customers using UPI**.

### Inventory Analysis

- Total inventory stock was **13,507 units**.
- **92 products** had sufficient stock.
- **8 products** were identified as requiring reorder.
- Total inventory value was **₹9,65,948.25**.
- **Cleaning** had the highest inventory value at **₹2,44,011**.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Maintain sufficient stock of high-selling products such as **Surf Excel**.
- Replenish the **8 products** identified as requiring reorder.
- Monitor the Cleaning category because it has the highest inventory value.
- Study the reasons behind strong August sales and consider applying successful practices to other periods.
- Continue supporting both Cash and UPI payment methods.
- Develop retention offers for regular customers.
- Consider targeted promotions for the large above-30 customer segment.
- Review lower-performing categories and consider suitable promotional strategies.
- Monitor inventory value regularly to avoid excessive capital being tied up in stock.
